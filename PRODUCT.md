product

## Users

Comic Scroller serves Chrome users who read manga on supported sites such as DM5, 8comic, Manhuagui, and Baozimh. They are usually in a reading flow, moving between chapters, checking followed titles, or returning to a previous place without wanting the source site chrome to interrupt the session.

## Product Purpose

Comic Scroller is a Manifest V3 Chrome Extension that redirects supported chapter pages into a cleaner reader, loads images in a continuous flow, remembers reading history, tracks followed series, and surfaces updates through a popup and manage page.

Success means readers can open a supported chapter, keep reading with minimal friction, return to the right place later, and manage local reading data without needing a backend service.

## Brand Personality

乾淨、直觀、明亮.

The interface should feel calm and task-focused. It should make reading and library management feel lighter, not more decorated.

## Anti-references

No explicit anti-references were specified.

Default avoidance: interfaces that feel cluttered, overly promotional, visually heavy, or decorative in ways that compete with the comic pages themselves.

## Design Principles

1. Reader first: page images and chapter flow take priority over interface decoration.
2. Local and trustworthy: history, follows, updates, import, and export should feel predictable because all user data lives locally.
3. Fast returns: popup and manage surfaces should help users resume or triage updates quickly.
4. Explicit data actions: destructive or cleanup flows must say exactly what is preserved and what is removed.
5. Quiet consistency: shared controls should look and behave the same across reader, popup, and manage surfaces.
